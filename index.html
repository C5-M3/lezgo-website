Road trips where nobody gets lost.

Lezgo keeps a group of cars together. The lead driver plans the route, and every car sees the same route and each other on the map.

Stop 1 of 4Before you leave

The leader plans the route once.

Pick a start point, add checkpoints for fuel or food, and set the destination. Lezgo draws the route the whole group will follow.

Stop 2 of 4In the Trip Room

Everyone joins with a 6-digit code.

Share the room code or an invite link. Each car joins with one phone, and the group checks the route and who is coming before setting off.

Stop 3 of 4On the road

Every car on one map.

Everyone follows the same turn-by-turn route. Tap a car to see its driver, street and arrival time.

Stop 4 of 4If something changes

A wrong turn gets caught right away.

Lezgo says it out loud and shows it at the top of the map, so the driver keeps their eyes on the road. If the leader changes roads, the route is recalculated for everyone.

Your trip, on your car's screen.

Connect your iPhone and the shared map moves to CarPlay, with the next turn and your arrival time. A Live Activity keeps the time left on your Lock Screen.

And a few more things.

Siri

Ask Siri about your trip while you drive.

No sign-up

Trips are shared through iCloud. No accounts to make and no ads.

Hand over the lead

The leader can pass the lead to another car without ending the trip.

New destination

Arrived early? The leader picks the next stop and everyone follows.

Planning a trip with friends?

Lezgo is free for iPhone. It needs an iCloud account and location access while the trip is open.
