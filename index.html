How a Lezgo trip works

  1. The leader plans the route

    Pick a start point, add checkpoints for fuel or food, and set the destination. Lezgo draws the route the whole group will follow.

  2. Everyone joins the Trip Room

    Share the room code or an invite link. Each car joins with one phone, and the group checks the route and who is coming before setting off.

  3. Drive with every car on the map

    Everyone follows the same turn-by-turn route and sees every car in the group. Tap a car to see its driver, street and arrival time.

  4. Nobody gets left behind

    If a car takes a wrong turn, its driver hears about it right away. If the leader changes roads, the route is recalculated for everyone.

Alerts you hear, not read

Drivers keep their eyes on the road. Lezgo speaks up when something changes, and one tap mutes it.

  • You left the route
  • You passed a checkpoint
  • You are ahead of the leader
  • The route was updated
