Questions

Does everyone in the car need the app?

No. One person per car joins the trip, usually the driver or the passenger holding the phone.

What if the leader has to leave?

The leader can hand over the lead first. If the leader leaves without doing that, the trip ends for everyone.

Do I need an account?

No. Lezgo uses your iCloud account and needs location access only while the trip is open.

Does it work with CarPlay?

Yes. Plug in your iPhone and the shared map appears on your car's screen.

Planning a trip with friends?

Lezgo is free for iPhone. It needs an iCloud account and location access while the trip is open.
