Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Email contact@joinlezgo.app. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened, and we will get back to you.

How do I start a trip?

Tap Lead, then plan the route: a start point, any checkpoints and the destination. Open the Trip Room and share the 6-digit room code or the invite link with the other cars.

How do I join a trip?

Tap the invite link, or open Lezgo, tap Join and type the 6-digit room code. Only one person per car joins.

The other cars do not see me on the map

Check that Lezgo can use your location in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Lezgo, and that your phone has an internet connection. Choose “Always” so the other cars still see you when your screen is off.

Lezgo says it cannot reach iCloud

Lezgo shares trips through iCloud. Sign in to iCloud in Settings, check your internet connection, then try again.

How do I leave or end a trip?

Members tap Exit Trip. The leader taps End Trip, which ends it for everyone. To leave without ending it, the leader hands over the lead first.
